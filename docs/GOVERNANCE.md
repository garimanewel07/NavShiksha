# Governance, inspections and insights (R6)

## Committees and decisions

**Where:** NES, Commands and principals: *Governance → Committees and decisions*. Anyone else who is
on a committee, or owns a decision or an action, sees *Governance → Committees and actions*.

### Committees

- A committee belongs to a level of the tree: NES (Executive, Management Advisory and Academic Advisory
  Committees, the NES Conference), a Command, or a school (School Managing Committee, Academic
  Monitoring, Purchase, Child Protection, Safety, or any other).
- Set up by NES, the Command or the principal, in one form: kind, terms of reference, quorum, chair,
  convener and members. Members are staff with a NavShiksha sign-in, or people outside it (a parent
  representative, a station nominee) kept by name. Special invitees attend but do not vote or count
  for the quorum.
- **Working group:** a committee for one topic, with a deadline.
- **Confidential** (always for Child Protection): only members see its agenda, minutes and decisions,
  not even NES unless they are a member.
- Terms end on a date; the record stays. A dissolved committee keeps its history.

### Agenda, meetings and minutes

1. Members submit agenda items before the meeting's cut-off (three days before, unless the convener
   sets another time). The convener accepts, defers or turns them down (with a reason the person
   is told). The convener's own items go straight on.
2. The convener schedules the meeting (venue, video link). Members are told, and reminded the day
   before. Accepted items and matters carried forward go onto it.
3. At the meeting the convener marks attendance. The quorum is counted (chair, convener and members
   present; invitees do not count). **Without a quorum no decision can be taken;** items can only be
   deferred or sent to another meeting.
4. The convener writes the minutes: an opening note, the discussion under each item, and each decision
   (with its owner, due date, actions and the schools it goes to, in one form).
5. The minutes are **circulated** to members, who comment. The **chair confirms** them (the convener
   does, if the chair has no NavShiksha sign-in). Confirmed minutes are locked; the database refuses
   any change. Minutes print as a PDF.

### Decisions

- On confirmation each decision takes its outcome: **decided**, **deferred**, or **needs another
  meeting**. The last goes onto the next meeting's agenda by itself.
- A decided matter goes as a **circular to each school it affects**; the principal acknowledges it
  and sends an **action-taken report**. Owners of the decision and its actions are told.
- **Live progress:** each action has an owner, a due date and a percentage; the owner reports what was
  done (with evidence files). The decision moves to *in progress* at the first update and to
  *implemented* when every action is done. Overdue actions remind their owner weekly.
- The convener or chair **closes** a decision, but only once every school has reported. A decision can
  also be sent back to the committee.
- **Resolution by circulation:** between meetings the convener puts a resolution to members, who vote
  for, against or abstain by a closing date. It is adopted when more vote for than against and enough
  members voted to make a quorum. The result is declared as soon as everyone has voted, or when voting
  closes.
- Decision numbers: `EC/2026-27/04` for NES committees, `SMC-NCS-MB/2026-27/02` for a school's.

### The NES Conference

A committee of kind *NES Conference*: its meetings are the **sessions**, its members the
**delegates**, its decisions the **resolutions**. Every principal sees it and submits their school's
topics (marked with the school's name), whether or not they are a delegate. Resolutions go to schools
and are followed through the year like any decision.

### Dashboard, waiting work and search

- **Decisions** (NES, Command, principal, for their part of the tree): open, overdue, implemented and
  closed; by committee and by owner; action-taken reports by school; coming meetings.
- **Waiting for me:** votes, minutes to confirm, agenda items to sort, decisions for my school, my
  actions, decisions I own, my meetings.
- **Search** finds decisions (by words or number) and confirmed minutes that the person may see.

## Inspections

**Where:** *Governance → Inspections*.

- **Checklists** are kept by NES (or a Command): sections, items, a weight per item, rated 1–3, 1–4 or
  1–5. Each inspection keeps a copy of its checklist, so editing a checklist never changes a report.
- NES or the Command **plans** an inspection of a school in its part of the tree: annual, thematic or
  **surprise**. The school is told in advance, except for a surprise visit, which it cannot see
  until the team starts it.
- The team **rates** each item (or marks it not applicable) with remarks, records **findings**
  (advisory, minor, major, critical; anything to put right has a date) and adds evidence files.
- The lead submits the **report** once every item is rated: a summary and the **score** (each rating
  as a share of the scale, weighted by the item's weight). Advice is filed as is; other findings stay
  open for the school.
- The principal **responds** to each finding; the inspector **accepts** (closing it) or sends it back
  with a note. When every finding is closed, so is the inspection. Reports print as a PDF.
- A principal can run a **self-assessment** of their school against any checklist.

## Insights

**Where:** *Governance → Insights* (principals: *My school against the network*).

- For every school, read live: students, attendance (30 days), roll taken (classes with the roll, 14
  days), fees collected of instalments already due, the Performance Index (latest year), posts vacant,
  staff signing in this week, last month's payroll, the latest inspection score and open findings,
  and decisions without an action-taken report.
- NES sees the network by Command, and each Command's schools; a Command sees its schools; each
  figure is coloured against the network average.
- **Early warnings** appear by themselves: attendance under 85%, roll taken for under 90% of classes,
  fees under 85% collected, last month's payroll not locked after the 7th, more than 10% of posts
  vacant, under 60% of staff using NavShiksha, overdue inspection findings, decisions without a
  report after 14 days.
- A **principal** sees their school beside the network average and where it stands ("2nd of 7"),
  without other schools' names.
- **MIS pack:** the same figures as a PDF for NES or a Command. On the 1st of each month NES and every
  Command are told it is ready.
- "Staff signing in" counts one row per person per day they sign in; nothing else about use is tracked.
