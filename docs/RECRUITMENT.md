# Recruitment, the Common Written Test, and help

How NavShiksha recruits teachers and staff for Navy Children Schools and Navy Kindergartens, how
NES runs the Common Written Test (CWT), and how every user gets help. The NavShiksha Assistant
answers questions from this page, so it is written for school staff, not for developers.

## Recruitment

**Candidates register once.** On NavShiksha Careers (`/careers`) a candidate gives their name,
date of birth, gender, category, email and mobile. A six-digit code goes to the email and another
to the mobile; both must be entered. There is no password: later they sign in with a code sent to
either. The profile (education with percentages, CTET/STET/TET, experience, documents such as
photo and signature) is kept for life. In later years they do not register again: they choose a
post, check the eligibility, confirm the declaration and pay the fee.

**A school posts a vacancy** under Recruitment → New vacancy. Choose the vacant sanctioned post
(post type, subject and pay level come from it) or the post type and subject. Eligibility starts
from NES norms for that post and can be adjusted:

| Post | Education | Test | Upper age |
|---|---|---|---|
| PGT | Post-graduation in the subject with 50%, B.Ed. | — | 40 |
| TGT | Graduation in the subject with 50%, B.Ed. | CTET or STET Paper II | 35 |
| PRT | Class XII with 50%, D.El.Ed. or B.Ed. | CTET or STET Paper I | 30 |
| Balvatika | Class XII with 45%, nursery teacher training (ECCE) | — | 30 |
| Principal / Vice Principal | Post-graduation with 50%, B.Ed., 10 / 8 years' experience | — | 55 (at least 35) |

The upper age is relaxed by 3 years for OBC, 5 for SC and ST, 10 for persons with disability and
5 for ex-servicemen; the minimum percentage is 5 points lower for SC, ST and PwD. Age is reckoned
on the closing date unless the school sets another date. Fees are set by category (none for SC,
ST, PwD and ex-servicemen by default; a lower fee for women can be set). The school may add
questions (yes/no or text). The vacancy is saved as a draft; **only the principal publishes it**.
Once published, only the closing date can be moved later and the description clarified.

**Applying.** The candidate sees every rule with a tick or a cross and the reason (for example
"M.Sc. 48%, needs 50%"). Only an eligible candidate whose documents are complete can apply: photograph, signature and left thumb impression (images), a photo ID, a certificate for every qualification and test in the profile (each uploaded against that degree or test), and the category, disability or ex-serviceman certificate if claimed. Registration verifies both the email and the mobile number with one-time codes. Then they apply, after
confirming the declaration. The application keeps a copy of the profile as it was. With a fee,
the application waits until it is paid; the fee goes to the school (receipt number, school's
books: gateway clearing to recruitment application fees, account 4170). NES receives no money.

**Screening.** Recruitment → a vacancy shows counts by stage and category. Filter by stage,
qualifying percentage, CTET/TET score, experience, age, city, gender, category, PwD,
ex-servicemen, Navy connection, written-test marks, a tag, or a name/number/email/mobile; sort by
merit, qualifying percentage, test score, experience, written test, interview, age, date or name.
Choose candidates with the tick boxes, or act on **everyone the filter matches** (thousands at a
time): shortlist, call for the written test, call for interview, waiting list, not taken forward;
the principal alone selects. Each candidate is told by email and SMS at every stage. "Email / SMS"
sends the school's own message to the chosen or matching candidates. "Spreadsheet" downloads the
matching list. A tag (for example "documents checked") can be added to many at once.

**Interviews.** "Schedule interview" gives each matching candidate a time slot in merit order
(minutes per slot, candidates per slot, venue, what to bring) and sends it by email and SMS; the
candidate confirms in their account. Marks out of 100 are entered on the application.

**Merit** = (written test × its weight + interview × its weight + qualifying percentage × its
weight) ÷ 100. Defaults: with a written test 60 / 30 / 10; without one 0 / 70 / 30.

**Appointing.** A selected candidate is appointed from the application: their staff record is
made from it (name, mobile, email, date of birth, gender) against the vacancy's post and
employment, so nothing is typed again. Pay, bank and classes are added on the staff profile. When
every post is filled the vacancy shows "Filled".

## The Common Written Test

1. **NES opens a test** (Common Written Test → New test): date, start time, minutes, the last
   date for schools' requirements and the last date for papers. Papers go to the centres 24
   hours before the start, by themselves.
2. **Schools send requirements.** On a vacancy with the written test, "Send to NES for the CWT".
   Vacancies for the same post and subject share one paper (for example TGT-MAT).
3. **Setter schools.** NES chooses three or four schools that have teachers serving in that post
   today (most first). A school testing candidates on the same paper is never offered.
   "Ask setters for every paper" does this for the whole test in one step. Principals and exam
   in-charges are told in NavShiksha and by email. Setter schools are not told which schools will
   use the paper.
4. **Uploading.** The setter school (principal or exam in-charge) uploads the question paper and
   answer key (PDF or Word) under "CWT papers to set", and can replace them until the due date,
   or decline with a reason. Files are encrypted as soon as they arrive.
5. **Review and choice.** NES opens each paper and key (every opening is recorded), rates them and
   chooses one per paper, with its maximum marks.
6. **Roll numbers and admit cards.** Schools move candidates to "Written test". NES gives roll
   numbers (CWT-2026-1/TGT-MAT/00001); each candidate gets an admit card by email and SMS and can
   download it (photo, signature, centre, reporting time, instructions).
7. **Release.** At the release time each centre's question papers, attendance sheets and a READ
   ME are put in one password-protected zip (AES-256, opens with 7-Zip or WinRAR). The password
   goes **only by SMS to the principal**; the file is downloaded in NavShiksha under "CWT centre".
   The principal can have the password sent again (three times at most). If something is missing
   at the release time, the NES CWT cell is told instead. Requirements and paper choices are
   frozen once released.
8. **After the test** the answer keys reach the centres the same way, for marking. Centres enter
   marks by roll number, or load the filled attendance sheet; marks become a score out of 100 in
   each candidate's merit, and schools take the next step with the usual filters (for example
   "written test at least 60").

## Help and support

Every user (staff, parents and candidates) has **Help**: choose what kind of help (something not
working, a wrong detail in a record, something extra, how do I, signing in), write a line and a
few sentences, and send. Record screens have "Report a problem with this record": choose the wrong
detail and what it should be. Do not write Aadhaar, bank or card numbers in a ticket.

**Who works on tickets.** The NavShiksha helpdesk and the NavShiksha Assistant (AI). They see the
ticket with names, phone numbers, emails and long numbers hidden, and attached records only as
references; they never see a school's records. The assistant works through a signed connection
that can only read tickets, reply, sort them and propose fixes; every call is logged.

**Fixes.**
- *A wrong detail* (a child's date of birth, a guardian's mobile, a staff member's blood group):
  the assistant proposes the correction; the office that keeps the record (for a child, the
  principal or the admission in-charge) sees the current and the new value under Approvals →
  Fixes from the helpdesk and approves or rejects it. Only then does NavShiksha make the change,
  through the same rules as the screens, in the approver's name.
- *Something not adding up* (designations, chat groups, a payroll draft, failed emails and SMS,
  bank matching, a child's transport fee): a repair that works things out again without changing
  anyone's data, approved in the same way.
- *A code change, a new module or anything unusual*: it goes to the developer first. Nothing
  changes until the developer accepts it and it is released.

The desk sorts tickets by agent and shows how many each resolved, how fast, and the users'
ratings. Users are told of every reply and can rate a resolved ticket.

**Email and SMS log** (principal and NES): every message NavShiksha sends, its state, and failed
ones retried at 1, 4, 9 and 16 minutes. Sign-in codes and CWT passwords are never shown there,
and their text is wiped once sent.
